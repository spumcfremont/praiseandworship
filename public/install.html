Put the songbook on your phone

No app store needed. It adds an icon to your home screen that opens straight into our songs.

iPhone or iPad

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen (top right on iPad).
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add. Look for the Worship icon on your home screen.

Not seeing "Add to Home Screen"? Tap Edit Actions… at the bottom of the share menu to add it.

Android

  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap the menu in the top right.
  3. Tap Install app or Add to Home screen, then Install.
  4. Look for the Worship icon on your home screen.

On a computer

In Chrome or Edge, click the install icon at the right end of the address bar. In Safari on a Mac, choose File → Add to Dock. To put it on your phone, open praiseteam.belongatstpaul.org/install on the phone.

You're all set

The songbook is installed. Open it from the Worship icon on your home screen.

First time you open it: enter the team password once. The app remembers you for 30 days.
